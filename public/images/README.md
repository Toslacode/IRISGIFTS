# Product photography

Filenames here must match the `image` field in `data/baskets.ts (basket images) and the story cards in components/home/GiftStories.tsx`,
for example `basket-bride-luxury.jpg`.

Until a file exists, the card renders a designed placeholder — a warm tinted
panel carrying the category mark — so the layout keeps its rhythm and nothing
shows a broken-image box.

Recommended: 1200×900 (4:3), JPEG or WebP, under 250KB, subject centred.
